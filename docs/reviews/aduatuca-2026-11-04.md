and 5.4 govern the executor's selections, partial execution and free actions.

origins and exact Warband quantities. A zero quantity selects an origin for

Ambush. German Warbands already at the destination participate. Unselected
origins remain unchanged. Multiple sources cannot absorb arrivals, because
their common destination cannot itself be selected as an adjacent source.

Only German Warbands may move, and each origin is selected once. Validation
rejects unavailable counts, negative/Boolean quantities, duplicate origins,
nonadjacent routes, destinations without a Fort and ungranted response fields.

ends without an impossible Battle.

Germans as the acting force. The Roman defender retains its own casualty
choices. Existing German Battle procedures and Caesar's exception remain in

encounter the new March decision; cross-revision exact replay is not promised.
